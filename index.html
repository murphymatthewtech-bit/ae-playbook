Account Playbook Generator

A tool I built for myself as a Mid-Market AE candidate — turns a company name into a sourced, structured deal-prep package: competitive battlecard, incumbent-tool detection, account SWOT, a MEDDPICC gap-checklist, an objection tree by buyer persona, and outreach drafts.

Why I built this

Every AE application asks you to prove you can research an account and think through a deal before you've had one. Most candidates answer that with a written paragraph. I built a repeatable process instead — a set of instructions I gave to Claude that runs the same rigorous research-and-synthesis workflow for any named company, every time, sourced rather than guessed.

It's not a deployed app that scrapes the web on its own — it's a defined workflow that runs inside a Claude session, because the research step (checking real job postings for tool signals, pulling competitor reviews, cross-referencing funding and product shape) needs real reasoning, not a canned script. What you can see below is a real output of that process.

How it works

  • Real research, not priors. Every non-obvious claim is sourced — company job postings, G2/Capterra reviews, funding databases. Anything uncertain is explicitly flagged as a hypothesis to confirm in discovery, not stated as fact.
  • Incumbent detection. The highest-value step: checking a target's own current job postings for the tool experience they're asking candidates to have — often the clearest public signal of what they already use or are evaluating.
  • MEDDPICC as a gap-checklist, not a script. Each element states what's actually known vs. what needs confirming in discovery.
  • Discovery and outreach style follows Kevin Dorsey's problem-bucket-question, disarming-cold-open, and ask-for-interest-CTA approach (Live Better. Sell Better. / 30 Minutes to President's Club) rather than generic template phrasing.
